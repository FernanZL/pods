You askedHow soon after waking should I get sunlight, and for how long?

Across the sleep episodes the advice is consistent: get outside within the first hour after waking1, aiming for roughly five to ten minutes on a clear morning and longer when it is overcast2. Viewing light through a window is described as far less effective1, and the same timing is linked to an earlier, steadier evening wind-down3.
